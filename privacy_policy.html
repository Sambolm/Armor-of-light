Privacy Policy

Game: The Shepherd: Armor of Light

Studio: Eternatones Studios

Effective Date: 2026-06-25

1. Overview

The Shepherd: Armor of Light is designed to be played directly in a web browser. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

2. Information We Collect

The game does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, precise location data, or other personally identifying information.

3. Local Save Data

The game may store progress, settings, Hope, region progress, armor progress, and audio volume settings in your browser using local storage. This information stays on your device/browser unless you clear it or your browser removes it.

4. Analytics and Hosting

If the game is hosted on a third-party platform such as GitHub Pages, that platform may collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, or request logs according to its own privacy policies.

5. Cookies

The game itself does not require cookies for core gameplay. Third-party hosting platforms may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

The game is family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should supervise children’s internet use and browser storage settings as appropriate.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent player personal information. Since the game does not intentionally collect personal information, there is no personal player data to sell.

8. Your Choices

You can clear saved game data by clearing your browser storage/site data for the game’s website.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the game evolves. The effective date will be updated when changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Eternatones Studios through the official project or studio contact channel.
