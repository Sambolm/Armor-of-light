Before You Begin

You are a shepherd of light. Find lost souls, protect them, and bring them safely home.

Soul Types
✧Fading Souls are newly rescued blue stars.
✦Comforted Souls generate more Faith after staying with you.
☀Blessed Souls give the strongest Faith and delivery bonuses.
◆Wounded Souls must be healed by standing near them.
♡Lonely Souls are rare and worth protecting.
Objectives

The objective tracker shows your next step: find the armor chest, rescue souls, and return to Sanctuary.

Sanctuary

The Sanctuary is your safe zone and delivery point. Its location may change each run. After opening the armor chest, Reveal Path guides you home.

Group Prayer

Stand still with followers to pray. Faith builds faster, but darkness is drawn toward the light.

Lantern Prayer Boost

Ancient lanterns strengthen prayer. Stand near a lantern with followers and begin Group Prayer to boost Faith generation to x3. Lanterns are placed differently in each region, so explore to find them. Every visible lantern is functional.

Bells

Stand still near a bell to restore it. Restoring a bell grants Faith and calls new souls into the region.

Armor Chests

Each region hides one chest. Stand on it for 3 seconds to receive a permanent Armor of God piece. Armor chests may appear in different places each run. Use Reveal Path if you need guidance.
