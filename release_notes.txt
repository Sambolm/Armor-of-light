The Shepherd: Armor of Light
Release Notes
Version: 1.0
Date: 2026-06-25

Overview
The Shepherd: Armor of Light is a Christian top-down action rescue game where the player carries light through dark regions, rescues lost souls, protects the flock, collects the Armor of God, and brings souls safely home to Sanctuary.

Version 1.0 Features
- 10 unique regions with increasing soul rescue goals.
- Sanctuary delivery system.
- Multiple soul types: Fading, Comforted, Blessed, Wounded, and Lonely.
- Armor of God progression.
- Staff of the Lamb reward after completing the armor set.
- Bell Tower restoration system.
- Lantern Prayer Boost system.
- Faith and Hope progression.
- Reveal Path ability.
- Radiance ability.
- Region music and sound effects.
- Heaven ending scene.
- Mobile landscape support.
- Dynamic joystick controls.
- Pause menu with music and SFX sliders.
- Persistent save/continue support.
- Post-game Level Select unlock.

Known Notes
- Best played in landscape orientation.
- Browser audio may require the first tap/click before music can begin.
- For the cleanest experience, use an updated browser.
